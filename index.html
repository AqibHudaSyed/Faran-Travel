FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Hajj, Umrah and our services answered.

When can Umrah be performed? +

Umrah can be undertaken at any time of the year except during the Hajj season (8th–13th Dhul Hijjah). It is highly recommended in Ramadan for maximum spiritual reward.

When is Hajj usually held? +

Hajj is performed annually from the 8th to 13th of Dhul Hijjah, the last month of the Islamic calendar. The exact Gregorian dates vary each year as the Islamic calendar is lunar.

What is the difference between Hajj and Umrah? +

Hajj is the fifth pillar of Islam — obligatory once in a lifetime for capable Muslims, performed on specific days. Umrah is a voluntary pilgrimage that can be performed any time of the year and carries great spiritual reward.

What documents are required for Umrah Visa? +

Valid Passport (min. 6 months), recent photos, completed application, meningitis vaccination proof. Women under 45 must be accompanied by a mahram (male guardian).

What is included in your Umrah packages? +

Umrah Visa, return flights from Lucknow, hotels in Makkah & Madinah, full board buffet meals, AC bus transport for Ziyarat, Zam Zam water (5 litres), laundry services, and Umrah kit.

How early should I book my Umrah package? +

We recommend booking 2-3 months in advance. For Ramadan packages, book even earlier as seats fill up very quickly.

Do you offer UAE Express Visa? +

Yes! UAE Instant Visa in 2-4 hours and Express Visa in 24 hours. Documents needed: Photo, Passport, Hotel Voucher, Round Trip Tickets, PAN Card.

Can solo women perform Umrah? +

Women above 45 can perform Umrah without a mahram in an organized group. Women below 45 must be accompanied by a mahram. Contact us for personalized guidance.
